# Bibliography check (26 Nov 2026)

`thesis/references.bib` is generated by `python scripts/build_bib.py` from `thesis/bib_overrides.json`. It holds every key cited in `thesis/0*.md`, **67 references** (IAAC asks for at least 20): 50 with a DOI and 17 without (policy documents, press, conference papers). `pandoc thesis/metadata.yaml thesis/0*.md --citeproc --bibliography thesis/references.bib --csl thesis/harvard-cite-them-right.csl` resolves all of them; its only warnings are the `@tbl:`/`@fig:` labels, which `thesis/layout/number_refs.py` resolves before pandoc.

## How verification was done
- **Crossref and OpenAlex could not be reached live.** The cloud proxy refuses `api.crossref.org` and `api.openalex.org` (HTTP 403), and so does the shell on Giovanni's PC. `doi.org`, Semantic Scholar, Europe PMC and DataCite are refused too.
- **OpenAlex (cached)** = the DOI, title, first author, venue and year match the OpenAlex record that `scripts/search_papers.py` stored when the paper was found (`notes/lit/candidates*.csv`, `shortlist.csv`; OpenAlex work ids there). Co-authors, volume and pages come from the literature matrix, which was filled from the papers read in full.
- **WebSearch** = the exact title was found on the publisher or a repository page (MDPI, PMC, university repositories). This confirms that the work exists and its venue. It does not confirm volume or pages.
- **Article numbers marked "from DOI pattern"** (Elsevier `…/j.xxx.YYYY.NNNNNN` → NNNNNN; Wiley `hyp.1NNNN` → eNNNNN) follow a pattern that holds for every entry in this list where the number is known. They are still inferred, not looked up.
- MDPI volume, issue and article come from the DOI (`f11101064` = 11(10):1064), which encodes them.
- **Nothing was invented.** Where a co-author's given name, the full author list, a volume or a page range is unknown, it is left out: the author list ends in "and others" (rendered "et al.") and the given name is missing.

**Fix for the gaps:** export the `THESIS_MACAD` Zotero collection with Better BibTeX on the PC (`thesis/README.md`). Then copy the full authors, volumes and pages of the entries flagged below into `thesis/bib_overrides.json` and rerun `scripts/build_bib.py`. Seven articles still render "[Preprint]" in the Cite Them Right style because both volume and pages are missing: baptista2018, bartens2008, pace2025, park2026, peng2026, silva2025, yang2019.

## Unresolved keys
None: every cited key has an entry. `hassan2017` (new in ch. 6) and `3cat2022` (ch. 3) were added from the chapter comment blocks. New keys are taken from a chapter's comment block (`key  #matrix-id`) as a fallback entry and printed as `CHECK`. Verify them, then move them into the overrides.

## Year conflicts
- **esbri2026:** the UB repository says 2025, but MDPI Atmosphere vol. 17 is 2026. Cited as 2026. Confirm on the MDPI page.
- **rahman2020meta:** online Dec 2019, issue 2020. Cited as 2020; the notes call it "Rahman 2019".
- **eslami2025:** the conference was held in Sept 2025, but OpenAlex dates the record 2026. Cited as 2025.
- **Issue year used** where the paper went online a year earlier: selbig2022, thom2022, coutts2016, kuehler2017, nytch2019, xiao2016.
