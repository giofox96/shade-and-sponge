| Trait coverage of top species | Same for both: leaf/wood traits OK (3TF); rooting depth partial (BROT 2 natives, GRooT); **interception missing** | same |

**Update 7 Oct (after literature review, see `notes/topic_decision.md`): Florence is now preferred.** Its pluvial flood type is documented, there is an HSG 1:10,000 layer, and there are hotspot maps (Pacetti et al. 2022). Bologna's Oct 2024 extents describe a mixed, culvert-driven event that trees cannot meaningfully affect. Original reading kept below for the record.

**Original reading:** Bologna has the stronger validation case: an observed, mapped, recent urban event plus soil Ksat. Florence has better terrain and rainfall-design inputs. Both share the same trait gap (interception, root architecture). On current evidence, **Bologna looks better for a validated thesis**, but only if the site is chosen where flooding came from **local runoff, not culvert overflow**. That needs checking against the Oct 2024 perimetration.
