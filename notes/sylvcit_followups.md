### §1 Real project: Santapau, Emili Roca and Desfar (Porta)
Three parallel streets inside Porta, our validation site, rebuilt in one municipal "reforma integral".
- Scope: about 5,500 m², EUR 2.3 M, wider sidewalks, sewer renewal, buried overhead cables and "una nova franja d'arbrat" (a new strip of trees). Six months of works, end planned for the second half of 2026 (district news, 19 Jan 2026: https://ajuntament.barcelona.cat/noubarris/ca/serveis-i-tramits/informacio-dobres/noticies/reforma-integral-dels-carrers-de-santa-pau-emili-roca-i-desfar-a-porta-1592495).
- Approvals (BOPB): Desfar executive project, initial approval 16 Sep 2025, EUR 557,352.07 incl. VAT (https://bop.diba.cat/anuncio/ver-pdf/3819788); Santapau, definitive approval by the Comissió de Govern 9 Oct 2025, EUR 1,101,205.00 incl. VAT (https://bop.diba.cat/anuncio/ver-pdf/3833557). No BOPB notice found yet for Emili Roca alone.
- Works: Desfar closed to traffic from 9 Mar 2026; Santapau from 16 Mar and Emili Roca from 30 Mar; expected end November 2026 (works notice: https://media-edg.barcelona.cat/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/17120639/Avis-Emili-Roca_Santapau-1.pdf).
- Today in our street-tree table (`exchange/to_gh/porta_street_trees.csv`): Santapau 6 trees, all Platanus × acerifolia; Emili Roca 0; Desfar 0. So the project plants trees on streets that have none. That is the tool's "Add trees" case, not plane replacement.
- What is missing: no published GIS perimeter and no planting plan or species list. The project files were on public display at https://seuelectronica.ajuntament.barcelona.cat/ca/exposicio-publica (30 working days from 30 Sep 2025, now closed). Perimeter can be drawn from the three street segments (Torrent de Can Piquer to Pg. Ciutat de Mallorca per the works notice; exact ends to confirm on the notice's plan). The species the city picks will show up in the open street-tree data after planting, so the use case can end with "tool vs city choice" once it is planted.
- Other Nou Barris projects seen, weaker fit: Pintor Alsamora green axis (Porta; neighbour demand and a plenary motion, no project, beteve 18 May 2024); Artesania (Guineueta, initial approval, works 2027, new trees, EUR 2.72 M); Carrer de la Jota (Vilapicina, EUR 626,000, no trees mentioned); Parc inclusiu de Can Dragó (Porta, park, start planned Aug 2026, per the Porta Consell de Barri summary of 12 Nov 2025).

### §5b What the three papers actually say (read 5 Nov 2026, cloud; PDFs not downloadable here, read through the web reader)
**Haase et al. 2014** (open access; full text read via Europe PMC, PMC3989520). Usable for the gap chapter, with care:
- Trade-offs: "Of all the studies under review, 23 (10 %) considered synergies in their analysis, and 43 (20 %) mentioned trade-offs" (section "Integration of UES Synergies and Trade-offs"). **Caution:** the section "The Temporal Dimension" says "none of the studies included in this review addressed trade-offs or synergies between the various UESs". The paper contradicts itself; cite the 10 % / 20 % figures with their section, not "none".
- Scale: the neighbourhood scale "seems to be underrepresented"; "only five papers presented results from more fine-grained analysis at a neighborhood or site scale"; "spatially explicit UES valuation at a relatively high resolution will be critical", so decisions can be prioritised "at the neighborhood or lot scale" (sections "Data and Models…", "The Temporal Dimension", "Emphasizing Spatially Explicit Approaches"). This confirms SylvCiT's paraphrase.
- Implementation: 48 studies address urban policy and planning, "only nine studies included more detailed recommendations"; "the general level of implementation is low" (section "The Practical Dimension").
- Not in it: no count for runoff regulation (local climate and air quality: 33 studies).
**Lin et al. 2019** (author copy: https://www.esf.edu/ere/kroll/documents/Lin_Kroll_Nowak_Greenfield_2019.pdf; page numbers below are the author copy's). The strongest support for the thesis gap:
- 242 papers and 476 case studies, 1996–2017; streets and parks are the most studied units; most common models i-Tree, ENVI-met, CFD, hedonic pricing (Abstract).
- "the majority of papers explore specific types of ES while ignoring the interaction among different ES" (Sec. 5.4.4, p.9); future work includes "investigating synergies and trade-offs" (Sec. 6, p.10).
- No model in the review estimates heat and stormwater together; stormwater appears only as separate categories (26 case studies on reduced runoff, Table 4, p.6). This is our inference from the review, not a sentence in it.
- "the spatial distribution of trees and their associated ES is essential" (Sec. 5.3.3, p.8); spatially explicit models listed: ENVI-met, CFD, SOLWEIG, i-Tree Design, i-Tree Landscape (Sec. 4.3, p.5).
**Esperon-Rodriguez et al. 2022** (open access per Semantic Scholar; full text not reachable from the cloud). Abstract only: climate analogues (a city's projected climate matched to another place's current climate) plus species vulnerability metrics, to flag at-risk species and suggest substitutes; Canadian cities as the case. Enough for one future-work sentence; read the PDF before citing any number.
