## 0. PDISBA: what the published volumes already hold (read 3 Nov)
PDISBA on BCNROC (https://bcnroc.ajuntament.barcelona.cat/items/3cc3df7e-be95-4111-a995-98715dbe172e, hdl 11703/119275,
licence **CC BY-NC-ND**): all 11 TXT layers downloaded by Giovanni to `databases/barcelona/raw/pdisba/` (gitignored),
renamed `01_memoria.txt` … `11_planols_vol5.txt`.
- **A T = 1 yr run exists for the current network.** Diagnosi §4.4.2 (Doc. 3, p.152): the 1D/2D model was run for
  T = 1, 10, 50, 100, 500 yr, present climate and RESCCUE 2100 climate. Results are in the map series, Diagnosi
  Taula 38 (p.179): **T = 1 → plànols 6.2** (present climate), 7.1 (climate change), 12.1/13.1 (with SUDS).
- Each series has 10 plates: 6.2.1 flows, 6.2.2 velocities, 6.2.3 network functioning, **6.2.4 surface drainage**,
  **6.2.5 pedestrian hazard**, 6.2.6 vehicle hazard, 6.2.7–6.2.8 risk, 6.2.9 damages, **6.2.10 detail plans**.
- Diagnosi Fig. 127 (p.271) charts the high-risk area for pedestrians per district (Nou Barris included) for T1–T500.
- The plates are raster maps inside the 1.7 GB Plànols PDFs (the TXT layer holds only the index); which volume holds
  series 6.2 is not yet known. **They are PDF maps, not GIS**, and ND forbids publishing modified versions.
- **Diagnosi PDF read (3 Nov, OMEN copy).** Fig. 59 (doc p.179 = PDF p.198), current network, present climate, metres
  of sewer by state: **T = 1: 1,628,413 m free-surface, 201,260 m pressurised, 10,004 m within 0.5 m of ground,
  45,982 m surcharging above ground**; T = 10: 757,601 / 618,324 / 63,931 / 445,803 m. So at T = 1 about 2.4% of the
  network surcharges to the surface, at T = 10 about 24% (shares computed here from the figure's table).
  §6.2.3 lists T = 10 critical points with maps; two are in or near Nou Barris: **6.2.3.11 Plaça Llucmajor** (old Riera
  de Sant Andreu, surface flow down c/ Garrofers; Fig. 71) and **6.2.3.12 Tajo – Cartellà** (c/ Cartellà drains the
  strip between Ronda de Dalt and Turó de la Peira; Fig. 72). Checked 4 Nov from Vol II: Llucmajor is on Porta's north-east edge, Tajo – Cartellà is outside Porta (see below).
- **Plànols PDFs: downloads incomplete** (Vol I 1.23 GB and Vol II 1.15 GB truncated, III/IV partial, V empty; pdfinfo
  cannot open any). Vol IV is complete (394 pp.) but holds series 12 (future + SUDS; p.100 = plate 12.2.10, sheet B04, 1:5000 grid). Series 6.2 is probably in Vol II. On 3 Nov BCNROC answered curl with a rate-limit page (`error_400_rateExceeded`): retry Vol II only, in the browser, another day.
- **Vol II read (4 Nov; complete copy, 467 pp., `Planols_-_Volum_II_meta.pdf`).** Pages identified by OCR of each
  title block: 6.1.x dry weather pp.7–77; **6.2 (T = 1, present climate, current network) pp.78–155**: 6.2.1–6.2.8
  general plans pp.78–85, 6.2.9 damages per barri pp.86–87, **6.2.10 detail plans pp.88–155** (68 sheets, A02…J09,
  alphabetical); 6.3.10 (T = 10) starts p.166 in the same sheet order (page = 6.2.10 page + 78). Legend: flooding
  0–0.02 / 0.02–0.04 / 0.04–0.06 / > 0.06 m, velocity > 1.8 m/s; pipes 1 free surface (green), 2 pressurised
  (yellow), 3 < 0.5 m below ground (orange), 4 above ground (red).
- **Porta lies on sheets H06, H07, I06, I07** (pp.140, 141, 146, 147 at T = 1; pp.218, 219, 224, 225 at T = 10),
  located by street names matched against the Porta tree-inventory streets (Alcúdia, Selva, Eduard Tubau, Lluc,
  Escultor Ordóñez, Garrofers, Maladeta, Pg Verdum, Pg Valldaura, Meridiana, Fabra i Puig). Not georeferenced.
- **What T = 1 shows in Porta (read from the plates, qualitative):** no surface ponding above 0.02 m anywhere inside
  Porta. The network goes into pressure (yellow) on parts of Av. Meridiana, Pg. Fabra i Puig and the C/ Lluc branch
  that runs to Pl. Llucmajor; the only above-ground (red) pipes are a few short ones inside the garden block between
  C/ Alcúdia and C/ Selva. The nearest T = 1 ponding is just outside Porta's north-west edge, at C/ Sant Isidre –
  C/ Peutret (Pg. Urrutia, sheet H07).
- **What T = 10 shows in the same place (contrast):** a continuous flooded strip from **Pl. Llucmajor down Pg.
  Valldaura**, C/ Lluc, C/ Escultor Ordóñez and C/ Garrofers (> 0.06 m in places, velocity > 1.8 m/s on the C/ Lluc
  branch at Llucmajor, red pipes), and a second strip along C/ Orduña – C/ Maladeta from Pg. Urrutia. So the Pl.
  Llucmajor critical point (Diagnosi 6.2.3.11) **is on Porta's north-east edge**; Tajo – Cartellà is not in Porta
  (C/ Cartellà runs under Turó de la Peira, sheet H07, outside the barri).
- **Meaning for the thesis.** At T = 1 the sewer in Porta copes: no street flooding, only a network close to capacity
  in a few trunks. Tree interception therefore acts on the volume entering a partly pressurised network, not on
  visible flooding; visible flooding starts by T = 10, where interception is a small share of the storm. Consistent with
  the "small gains" framing. Usable in the thesis as a qualitative statement with citation to PDISBA plates 6.2.10 /
  6.3.10, sheets H06–I07. The plates are CC BY-NC-ND: crops stay on the OMEN (`raw/pdisba/fig/v2/`), not in the repo.
- **Consequence for the BCASA request:** no need to ask *whether* a T = 1 run exists; ask for the **GIS layers of
  plànols series 6.2 (and 6.3, T = 10) for Nou Barris**, plus the gauge series.

## 6. Underground services (sewer, electricity, gas, water, telecom): how to get them (searched 11 Nov)

**Why.** Tutor feedback F3 / A3 (`notes/tutor_feedback_10nov.md`): buried networks may decide where an *added* tree can go. Replacements reuse an existing pit, so the conflict was settled when the pit was built; the data matters for added spots only.

**Bottom line.** Barcelona has no open underground-network data, but it does have a **one-stop portal**: **eWise**, run by **ACEFAT**, the body that coordinates utility works in Barcelona ("Coordinació i gestió d'obres de serveis a la ciutat de Barcelona"). One request returns the existing services of every connected company in a drawn area. It is the route that works from Italy, by email, in days rather than months. Everything else is slower, partial, or closed to us.

### 6.2 Proxies we can use now (sourced rules, no request needed)

- **Sewer.** BCASA's *Guia de criteris tècnics generals de la xarxa de clavegueram* (v01, 30 Sep 2015): sewers "usualment discorre col·lineal amb l'eix de la calçada" (§3.1, p.3); **"No es permet el pas de xarxes de clavegueram sota línies d'arbres"** (§3.1 crit. 19, p.6); minimum cover 1.60 m under sidewalk, 1 m under carriageway (crit. 16, p.5); sewer deeper than every other service (crit. 17); each new house connection gets a manhole **on the sidewalk**, 40×40 cm up to 2.5 m deep, 70×70 cm deeper (§3.5.1 crit. 7, p.17); gullies on the gutter lines and upstream of pedestrian crossings (§3.3.b, p.9); separations to other services 50–70 cm recommended, 20–30 cm minimum (crit. 18 table, p.6). So the sewer main is not the constraint for a sidewalk pit; **house connections** are, and they run perpendicular from each building.
- **Pits over services.** Parcs i Jardins *Plec de prescripcions tècniques per al disseny, l'execució i la recepció d'espais verds* (PTEV, rev. May 2024) §2.2.6.2, p.31: "no es podran situar parterres ni escocells a tocar de façanes ni sobre infraestructures soterrades existents" unless waterproofing and drainage are designed. §3.2 "Protecció dels serveis existents" (p.82) and §5.3.9 "Escocells" (p.116) were **not readable from the cloud**; read them on the PDF from the OMEN before using any distance.
- **Possible tool rule (ASSUMPTION until §3.2 is read):** exclude added spots within a buffer of each building entrance (house connections of water, gas, electricity and sewer cross the sidewalk there) and of mapped lamps and chambers, as done for crossings and bus stops in `scripts/porta_spot_exclusions.py`. Report added spots as an upper bound either way.
